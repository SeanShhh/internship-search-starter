# Role assessment

## Why this is here

This file makes prioritization visible before time is spent on tailoring. It records what the role asks for, what the user can support, and what remains uncertain.

## Example assessment

- Fit: potential fit for someone with evidence of organized project work, communication, and curiosity about how products are built.
- Eligibility: unknown because the posting is fictional.
- Priority: medium until a real role and constraints are verified.
- Evidence to look for: coordinating a project, analyzing feedback or data, explaining a decision clearly.
- Missing evidence: no fictional candidate claims are supplied. Ask the user for a real example rather than drafting one.
- Duplicate check: no duplicate found in the fictional tracker.
