# Evidence-based resume and materials

Use before drafting a resume, cover letter, or application response.

Read the target role, `private/evidence-bank.md`, relevant story menu, writing guide, and any current resume. Explain the role's highest-value criteria and the two or three complementary evidence items selected to address them. Ask focused questions when a required claim lacks evidence.

Draft only claims supported by the evidence bank or user-provided material. Keep a claim-to-evidence reference in the application's `claim-map.md`. Do not add metrics, accomplishments, tools, scope, or eligibility details without support.

For resumes, use the flexible template sections—education, experience, projects, leadership, and skills—and include only sections that strengthen the application. Default to one page. Produce editable Word and PDF files when the environment can render them; otherwise save the editable source and record rendering as an outstanding check.

Before marking materials ready, review factual accuracy, selected evidence, role relevance, stale employer or role names, and rendered overflow. Ask the user to review and submit applications themselves.
